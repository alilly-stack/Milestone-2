01 / THE BIG PICTURE

Why We Look
to the Stars

For thousands of years, humans have looked up at the night sky with wonder. Astronomy allows us to explore our origins, search for life beyond Earth, and understand the vast universe we call home.

With powerful telescopes, spacecraft, and international research, scientists continue to uncover the secrets of planets, stars, galaxies, and the forces that shape them.

The James Webb Space Telescope

The James Webb Space Telescope observes the universe in infrared light, helping scientists study the earliest galaxies, forming stars, and atmospheres of distant planets.

Worlds Beyond Our Solar System

Thousands of exoplanets have been identified orbiting other stars. Their variety helps researchers understand how planetary systems form and where life might be possible.

Gravitational Waves

Scientists have detected ripples in spacetime created by powerful cosmic events, such as collisions between black holes. This gives us a new way to observe the universe.
