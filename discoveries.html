Milestones in astronomy

Major scientific discoveries

These milestones changed how people observe space and explain the universe. They also show how scientific knowledge develops over time.

Observation

Heliocentric model

The heliocentric model placed the Sun near the center of the planetary system and helped reshape explanations of planetary motion.

New technology

The telescope

Telescopic observations revealed details that could not be seen with the unaided eye, expanding the evidence available to astronomers.

Cosmic scale

Galaxies beyond the Milky Way

Observations of other galaxies showed that the Milky Way is only one of many galaxies in a much larger universe.

Modern evidence

Expansion of the universe

Evidence that distant galaxies are moving away from one another supports the conclusion that the universe is expanding.

Early universe

Cosmic microwave background

This faint radiation is important evidence for the hot, dense early state of the universe described by the Big Bang model.

Ongoing exploration

Exoplanets

Thousands of planets have been identified around other stars, helping scientists investigate how planetary systems form and evolve.

Science keeps moving forward

Discoveries are refined as instruments improve and researchers test explanations against new observations. A scientific model is strongest when it continues to fit the evidence.
