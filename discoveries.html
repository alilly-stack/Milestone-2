How We Study Space

Each method gives researchers a different way to investigate distant objects and events.

James Webb Space Telescope

Webb observes infrared light, which helps scientists study very distant galaxies, star formation, and the atmospheres of exoplanets. Its observations add detail to questions about how the universe developed.

INFRARED ASTRONOMY
02
◎

Exoplanets

Exoplanets are planets outside our solar system. Scientists often find them by measuring the small dip in a star's brightness as a planet passes in front of it, or by observing a star's motion.

PLANETARY SYSTEMS
03
〰

Gravitational Waves

Gravitational waves are ripples in spacetime produced by accelerating massive objects. Their detection allows scientists to study events such as black-hole mergers in a new way.
